How to Play

  • ← → or A D run · Space / ↑ / W jump
  • Speed = height. Build a run-up and your capy jumps way higher. At full tilt it cartwheels.
  • Ricochet off the walls: hit a wall in mid-air and you fly back the other way at full speed. Steer the new way straight away to keep it. Jump right as you reach a wall to kick off it.
  • Combos: chain jumps of 2+ floors before the meter on the left runs out. Score = 10 × floor + combo².
  • The tower starts moving at floor 5 and speeds up every 30 s. Drop off the bottom and it's over.
  • P pause · M mute · N next track
